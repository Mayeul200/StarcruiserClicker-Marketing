## Description longue
**Starcruiser Clicker** is a free incremental/clicker game playable instantly in
your browser — no download, no account (PWA installable on PC & mobile).

- **Click** the golden part to earn Parts
- **Build** 14 structures, from the humble Workshop to the industrial Black Hole
- **Assemble your rocket** piece by piece (10 parts, +4% range each)
- **Launch** expeditions through a real cinematic and reach 11 destinations:
  Moon, Mars, Neptune, Pluto, Oort Cloud, Proxima Centauri, Sirius, the Milky
  Way center, Andromeda, the Virgo Cluster — each with a permanent production bonus
- **Prestige** with Stardust to unlock 57 permanent upgrades in the Galactic Workshop
- **Contracts, comets, card boosters, 110 trophies** and a **worldwide leaderboard**
- After the Virgo Cluster: **infinite mode** — double your record again and again
- **7 languages**: FR, EN, ES, DE, IT, PT, NL
